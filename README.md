# Block Puzzle

A drag-and-drop block puzzle for phones. Drag pieces from the tray onto the
8x8 board; full rows and columns clear. The game ends when none of your
pieces fit.

By **RisingForce**.

## Play

**Play it here: https://thekillergame.github.io/block-puzzle/**

Or open `index.html` in any browser, on a phone or a computer. It's a single file
with no install, no server and no internet needed (the fonts are embedded and
the sounds are made in code). The piece you drag floats above your finger so
you can always see it, a ghost shows where it lands, and lines it would
complete light up.

## Features

- Scoring: points for every block placed, more for clearing several lines at
  once, a combo multiplier for clearing on back-to-back placements, and +300
  for clearing the whole board
- Special blocks: stars (+50 and a coin when cleared) and bombs (clear a 3x3)
- Tools: every game starts with 1 rotate and 1 shuffle; if you get stuck with
  a tool left, the game waits and nudges you to use it
- Daily challenge: the same piece sequence for everyone, generated from the
  date, with your best score for the day
- 6 block skins, each with its own look and power, unlocked by best score:
  candy, jelly (1,000: combos survive one miss), gem (2,500: more stars),
  ice (5,000: more bombs), neon (7,500: +1 rotate) and gold (10,000: double
  coins)
- Coins (1 per 100 points, plus stars), 13 missions, a store with extra
  rotates and shuffles and 6 board themes, and credits
- Saved: best score, daily bests, stats, coins, missions, skin, store items, mute
